How to use Proxymancer
📋 Paste a decklist

Paste your deck in Arena or MTGO format: quantity followed by card name, one per line. You can also specify a set and collector number.

Example
1 Sol Ring
4 Lightning Bolt
1 Black Lotus (VMA) 4
1 Tymna the Weaver

Then click Sync Cards to load the images.

🎨 Choose alternative arts

Click any card in the grid to open the Print Catalog. There you can browse all available printings filtered by language and year.

Once you pick an art and confirm, that card is pinned to that exact printing — it will be remembered even if you reload the deck.

Use Update to Latest Arts to reset all cards to their most recent printing in the selected language.

🖱️ Drag & drop from Scryfall / EDHREC

You can drag any card image directly from Scryfall or EDHREC and drop it onto the card grid or the decklist textarea — the card will be added automatically.

If the card already exists in your deck, its quantity increases by 1 instead of adding a duplicate.

🪙 Add tokens from Scryfall

To add a token, go to scryfall.com, search for the token (e.g. Treasure Token), and drag its image to the grid.

You can also type it in the decklist using the T: prefix:

Example
1 T:Treasure
1 T:Goblin
🖼️ Add custom card images

Click Add Custom Card to upload your own images (PNG, JPG, WebP). They will appear in the grid and be included in the PDF.

Custom cards are not linked to Scryfall, so they won't appear in the decklist textarea and won't be affected by Sync or language changes.

📄 Configure the PDF

Scale: Real Size prints at exact MTG card dimensions (63×88mm). Fit to Page scales cards to fill the sheet.

Bleed: Adds 2mm around each card so cuts don't leave white edges. Recommended for guillotine cutting.

Card gap: Space between cards. Minimal (0.3mm) works best with cut marks.

Cut marks: Thin lines at the corners of each card to guide cutting. Enable together with a small gap for best results.

Quality: Higher quality = larger file. Normal (300 DPI) is the best balance for printing.

💾 Save and restore your deck

Proxymancer automatically saves your decklist and pinned arts in your browser's local storage every time you make a change.

Use the Load Previous Deck button to restore the last saved deck, including all art choices.

⚠ Clearing your browser data will erase saved decks. To keep your work safe, copy the decklist text to a file.
